# Zygn ERP Software

**ERP and business management software for interior designers and design agencies**

Zygn is an interior design ERP and project management platform designed to help interior designers, design studios, contractors, and design agencies manage projects and business operations from one centralized system.

Zygn brings together project planning, CRM, BOQ and estimation, procurement, task management, billing, accounting workflows, and client management.

## What Zygn Helps Manage

### Project Management

Plan and manage interior design projects, organize activities, assign tasks, monitor progress, and coordinate teams throughout the project lifecycle.

### CRM & Lead Management

Manage enquiries, leads, client information, follow-ups, and client-related activities in an organized workflow.

### BOQ & Estimation

Create and manage bills of quantities, estimates, materials, quantities, rates, and project cost information.

### Procurement Management

Manage material requirements and purchasing activities while keeping procurement connected with project execution.

### Task & Team Management

Create tasks, assign responsibilities, track progress, and coordinate work across different project stages.

### Billing & Accounting

Manage billing activities, project expenses, payments, and financial information related to interior design projects.

### Client Management

Keep client information, project communication, requirements, approvals, and related activities organized.

## Interior Design Project Workflow

Zygn supports a connected workflow from initial enquiry through project completion:

**Lead → Client → Project → Design → BOQ & Estimation → Procurement → Execution → Billing → Handover**

Centralizing these activities can help design teams reduce fragmented information and maintain better visibility across projects.

## About ERP for Interior Design Businesses

Interior design businesses often manage several connected activities at the same time, including client enquiries, project planning, material estimation, procurement, execution, billing, and client communication.

An ERP-oriented system can bring these business processes together and provide a centralized view of operational information.

Zygn combines ERP-oriented business workflows with interior design project management capabilities to support the needs of design-focused businesses.
